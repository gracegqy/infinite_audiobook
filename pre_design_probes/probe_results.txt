PROBE 2 — Chunked synthesis: seams + offset accuracy
Status: OFFSETS VERIFIED · Grace seam-listen PENDING
Script: probe2_chunked_offsets.py · Outputs: data/interim/probe2/

- 6 Usher paragraphs rendered separately, concatenated two ways:
  concat_A_buttjoin.wav (raw butt-join) and concat_B_200ms_gaps.wav (200ms silence).
- Offsets = cumulative chunk sample counts. Verified two ways:
  (a) construction: concat length == sum of chunk lengths (1,829,400 samples exact);
  (b) independent energy check at each offset in variant B: 150ms window before each
      offset is silence (-120 dB), 500ms after is speech (-25..-29 dB) — 6/6 OK.
  Offset error is 0 by construction, far inside the ~100ms budget.
- Kokoro chunks end in digital silence (tail RMS < -200 dB), so butt-joins should be
  clean; paragraph-final prosody sounds natural in isolation.
- GRACE: listen to concat_A_buttjoin.wav — are the 5 seams audible/annoying? If yes,
  design will insert ~200ms gaps (variant B), which costs nothing and offsets stay exact.
